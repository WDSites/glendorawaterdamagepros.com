Hardwood Floor Repair

Wood Floor Water Damage in Glendora: Cupping, Crowning and What Repair Really Takes

By the Glendora Water Damage Pros team · Published October 27, 2026

☎ (626) 879-0276See all services

Hardwood that drinks up water swells, and the swelling shows as cupped, crowned or lifted boards. In Glendora homes it often starts small: a washer supply valve that lets go, or an AC condensate line that backs up and drips against the wall base for weeks.

Many floors can be dried in place if the work starts early. The wrong repair, usually sanding too soon, can ruin a floor that was recoverable. This post covers the stages of damage, the meter readings that matter, and the point where replacement makes more sense.

Cupping, crowning and buckling are three stages of one problem

Cupping comes first. The edges of each board rise above the center, so the floor looks like a row of shallow troughs. It usually means moisture reached the boards from below or from the side, and the underside swelled faster than the top.

Crowning is the reverse, with the center of each board higher than the edges. It shows up when a cupped floor was sanded flat before it dried, or when water sat on top of the boards. Buckling is the last stage, where whole sections push up off the subfloor, sometimes an inch or more.

A cupped floor is often recoverable. A buckled floor has usually lost its fastening to the subfloor, and the odds of saving it drop sharply. The difference between those two is mostly how many days the wood stayed wet.

Drying hardwood in place or pulling it up

Drying in place uses drying mats or panels laid over the floor. A vacuum pulls air through the board seams while heated air moves across the surface, drawing moisture out of both faces of the wood. The setup usually runs for several days, and technicians check it daily with a moisture meter.

This works best on solid or thick engineered hardwood over a wood subfloor, when the boards are still tight and the water source was clean. It works poorly on glued-down floors over concrete, thin laminates, or any floor with a layer of foam underlayment that holds water.

Replacement is the better call in a few situations:

  • Boards have lifted or split along the tongue and groove.
  • The floor stayed wet for well beyond the 24 to 48 hour window the EPA uses as its guideline.
  • The EPA limits its drying advice to clean water, so the water type has to be known first.
  • Readings in the subfloor stay high after several days of drying.

Two real scenarios: a washer valve and a condensate line

One homeowner heard a hiss from the laundry closet and found the washer hot-water valve had cracked. About a gallon a minute ran across the closet floor and under the baseboard into the hallway oak before the main was shut. Because the water was clean supply water and the boards were still tight a day later, the floor was dried with mats and most of it was kept.

The second case was slower. An air handler in a hall closet had a clogged condensate line that overflowed a little each day for what turned out to be a couple of months. By the time the boards cupped, the subfloor under them read high and several boards were stained at the seams. Those boards were replaced and the rest dried and blended.

Moisture meter readings, in plain terms

Hardwood in a lived-in home normally reads somewhere around 6 to 9 percent moisture content. A wet floor can read 15 percent or more, and water-soaked boards can pass 25 percent. Pin meters read at depth, and pinless meters scan a wider area without leaving holes.

The numbers only mean something next to a reference. A technician reads a dry board from a room that was not affected, then compares the wet area to that baseline. The goal is not zero but a match with the dry reference.

Readings should also be taken in the subfloor and sometimes the wall base. A thermal imaging camera can show a cold, wet patch hiding behind a good-looking surface. Ask for the actual numbers and where they were taken.

Why sanding too early fails

Wet wood is swollen wood. If you sand a cupped floor flat while the boards still hold extra moisture, you remove the high edges, and when the wood finishes drying the center ends up higher than the edges. That is crowning, and it is far harder to fix than the cupping was.

Finish also traps moisture. Applying stain and sealer over boards that have not stabilized can cloud the coating and lock dampness in. A sensible schedule waits until readings match the dry reference and have held steady, and the wood can keep moving for a couple of weeks after it reads dry. Refinishing after that gives a flat surface that stays flat.

The Azusa city site, in its page on Azusa Light & Water, says that utility serves every Azusa customer and also parts of Covina, Glendora, Irwindale and West Covina. For flooring jobs that start with a washer or toilet supply line, the quickest fix is usually the angle stop at the fixture, and the meter is the backup.

First-day checklist for a wet wood floor

These steps protect the floor while you decide what to do next.

  1. Stop the source

    Close the fixture shutoff or the main valve, and unplug anything standing in water only if the outlet and the floor around it are dry.

  2. Remove standing water and furniture

    Mop or wet-vacuum puddles, then lift rugs, boxes and furniture legs off the boards. Wet rubber or foam pads stain wood quickly.

  3. Photograph everything

    Shoot wide and close, with a coin or tape measure for scale, covering the wet patch, the source and any cupping.

  4. Book a moisture assessment

    Ask for readings in the boards and the subfloor before anyone proposes a repair, and ask to see the numbers.

Honest limits

Drying will not flatten a floor that has already buckled, and it does not repair a plumbing leak. A plumber fixes the source, and a flooring contractor handles sanding and finishing. Our part is the drying and the readings that tell everyone when the floor is ready.

If you need a repair scope for a claim, our crews can write one from the readings, and water damage repair work begins there.
