About Glendora Water Damage Pros

A 24/7 dispatch line for water damage and mold in Glendora, CA.

☎ (626) 879-0276See all services

Glendora Water Damage Pros is a water damage restoration and mold remediation line for Glendora, CA and the neighboring cities of San Dimas, Azusa and Covina. The number answers 24 hours a day, and calls are sent to licensed and insured restoration professionals.

What we do

We remove standing water, dry wet structure, clean up contaminated materials, rebuild damaged surfaces and handle mold when moisture has let it start. The services page lays out which service answers which situation.

Scope and price are explained before work starts. We use moisture meters, thermal imaging cameras, air movers, LGR dehumidifiers and extraction units, and we describe each in plain words so you know what is running in your house and why.

What we do not do

Water damage restoration is not waterproofing or foundation repair. If water keeps coming in from the ground, the fix belongs to a drainage or foundation specialist, and we will say so. We also do not promise insurance outcomes. Coverage is between you and your insurer.

Where we are
