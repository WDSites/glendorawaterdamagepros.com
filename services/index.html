Water Damage Services in Glendora, CA

Six services, one 24/7 dispatch line, and a plain guide to which one you need.

☎ (626) 879-0276See all services

Glendora Water Damage Pros covers the full run of a water loss in Glendora, CA, from the first extraction to the final coat of paint. Each service below has its own page because each answers a different question, and the right one depends on what is happening in your house right now.

Start with the situation. If water is standing, call and ask for extraction. If the water is gone but the walls are wet, you need drying. If everything is dry but damaged, you need repairs.

The six services

Water Damage Repair

The rebuild: new drywall, insulation, flooring, paint and cabinets once readings show the structure is dry.

Read the water damage repair page →

Flood Damage Restoration

Floodwater from storms, burn-area mud, groundwater or a sewer backup, where contamination changes the rules.

Read the flood damage restoration page →

Water Mitigation

Damage control in the first days, with shutoffs, extraction, air movers and daily moisture checks.

Read the water mitigation page →

Water Damage Cleanup

Hauling out soaked debris, sanitizing surfaces and clearing odor after the water source is stopped.

Read the water damage cleanup page →

Emergency Water Removal

Submersible pumps and extractors for deep standing water, wet carpet and flooded crawl spaces.

Read the emergency water removal page →

Mold Remediation

Containment, removal and clearance checks for mold that moisture has allowed to spread.

Read the mold remediation page →

Which service fits your situation

What you seeService that fitsWhy
Standing water on the floorEmergency Water RemovalExtraction units remove the volume before drying starts
Wet walls, floors or ceiling after the leak stoppedWater MitigationAir movers and dehumidifiers dry the structure in days
Soaked carpet, ruined drywall, contaminated itemsWater Damage CleanupWet debris is removed and the space is sanitized
Storm runoff, mud, sewage or groundwaterFlood Damage RestorationContaminated water needs protective equipment and containment
Dry but damaged ceiling, wall or floorWater Damage RepairRebuilding comes after moisture readings reach dry standards
Musty smell, dark spots, a stain that returnsMold RemediationMoisture lets mold grow, and containment keeps it from spreading

Not sure which one fits? Call the 24/7 line and describe the room. Dispatch will sort out the right service and explain the scope before anyone starts work. Mold calls are a large share of what we hear, and the Mold Remediation page goes into the most detail.
